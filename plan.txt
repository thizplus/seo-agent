✍️ STEP 2 — Article Generator (Core จริง)

คุณ “ต้องทำตัวนี้ให้โหดก่อน”

Flow:
keyword → SERP analyze → generate outline → write → schema → image → done
Prompt structure (ของจริงที่ควรใช้):
prompt = f"""
You are an SEO expert.

Keyword: {keyword}

SERP data:
- avg word count: {serp['avg_word_count']}
- intent: {serp['intent']}
- headings: {serp['common_headings']}

Task:
Write a better article than top 10 results.

Requirements:
- 2000+ words
- clear H1-H3 structure
- include FAQ section
- include internal linking suggestions
- strong EEAT
- Thai language
"""

    add_internal_links()
